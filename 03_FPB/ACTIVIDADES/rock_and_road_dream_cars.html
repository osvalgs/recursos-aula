Stop 2 · how to play

Classic Car Trumps

  1. Shuffle and share the cards. Look only at your top card.
  2. Player 1 chooses a stat and reads it: “Top speed: 195 km/h.”
  3. The others read the same stat on their card.
  4. The highest number wins. Say the phrase: “My car is faster!” and take the cards.
  5. The winner chooses the next stat. The player with more cards at the end wins.

No English, no cards!
